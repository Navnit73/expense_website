At its simplest, financial health boils down to one fundamental equation:

`Net Cash Flow = Total Income - Total Expenses`

If Net Cash Flow is consistently positive, you build wealth, increase investment reserves, and expand business resilience. If Net Cash Flow is negative, debt accumulates and risk escalates.

---

## The Income Dimension: Tracking Diverse Revenue Streams

Income is rarely a single monolithic number. In modern freelance and creator economies, income streams frequently include:

- **Primary Salary / Wages**: Predictable bi-weekly or monthly disbursements.
- **Freelance & Client Retainers**: Variable amounts with fluctuating payout dates.
- **Dividends & Capital Gains**: Passive investment returns.
- **Rental / Royalty Income**: Asset-backed cash flows.

## Cash Flow Scenarios & Corrective Actions

| Scenario | Status | Diagnostic Indicator | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Income > Expenses** | **Healthy Surplus** | Savings rate > 20% | Direct surplus into index funds or high-yield reserves |
| **Income = Expenses** | **Break-Even** | Savings rate 0-5% | High vulnerability to emergencies; audit recurring expenses |
| **Income < Expenses** | **Deficit / Burn** | Savings rate negative | Immediate spending freeze on non-essential categories |
