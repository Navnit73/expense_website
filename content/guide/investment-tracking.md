## Why Track Investments Alongside Daily Cash Flow?

When expense tracking and investment tracking live in separate silos:
1. You cannot easily calculate your real **Monthly Capital Allocation** (how much surplus converted into wealth-building assets).
2. You risk emotional rebalancing rather than systematic dollar-cost averaging.
3. Your net-worth trajectory remains fragmented across multiple brokerage logins.

## The 3 Rules of Disciplined Asset Allocation

### 1. Automate Monthly Investment Contributions
Treat investments as a non-negotiable monthly "expense" to your future self. Log investment transactions on the exact day automated transfers clear.

### 2. Monitor Portfolio Growth vs Expense Growth
Your ultimate financial milestone is the crossover point where portfolio returns sustainably cover baseline living expenses. Expenseliy's net asset snapshot visualizes this gap in real time.

### 3. Maintain Calm Discipline
Avoid checking market tickers hourly. Focus on systematic contribution tracking and long-term asset growth.
