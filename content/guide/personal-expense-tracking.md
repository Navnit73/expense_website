Most personal finance advice starts with restrictive rules like "stop buying coffee" or "cut all entertainment." In reality, lasting financial freedom comes not from deprivation, but from **awareness**.

When you track your personal expenses accurately, you gain the superpower to spend intentionally on what brings you joy while eliminating money leaks that offer zero value.

---

## The 50/30/20 Rule & Expense Tracking

A powerful framework to pair with daily expense tracking is the classic 50/30/20 allocation:

- **50% Needs (Fixed Costs)**: Rent/mortgage, groceries, essential utilities, insurance, minimum debt payments.
- **30% Wants (Discretionary)**: Dining out, hobbies, streaming subscriptions, vacations, shopping.
- **20% Savings & Debt Acceleration**: Emergency fund contributions, investment portfolio funding, retirement accounts.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     TOTAL NET INCOME                        |
+------------------------------+---------------+--------------+
|        50% Needs             |   30% Wants   | 20% Savings  |
|  (Housing, Utilities, Food)  | (Dining, Fun) |(Investments) |
+------------------------------+---------------+--------------+
```

Without tracking, most people assume their discretionary spending is around 15%, only to discover upon logging that it exceeds 45%.

## 3 Key Steps to Start Today

### 1. Document Fixed Baseline Costs First
Write down all unavoidable monthly expenses:
- Housing (Rent/Mortgage)
- Utilities & Internet
- Transportation / Commute
- Core Insurance & Medical

### 2. Track Variable & Discretionary Purchases Daily
Variable spending (groceries, dining, spontaneous purchases) requires daily awareness. Using Expenseliy on your mobile browser or laptop allows you to record a transaction in 5 seconds right after paying.

### 3. Review Your Weekly "Burn Rate"
Look at your category distribution every Sunday evening. If dining out or entertainment is trending high by mid-month, you can naturally adjust before the month closes.
