Every dollar of legitimate deductible expense that goes unrecorded is money handed directly to tax authorities. For self-employed individuals, working professionals, and independent contractors, proactive expense tracking is the difference between thriving margins and unexpected tax liabilities.

---

## What Qualifies as a Deductible Expense for Self-Employed?

In most jurisdictions (including the US IRS, UK HMRC, Australian ATO, and Canadian CRA), a deductible self-employed expense must satisfy two fundamental tests:

1. **Ordinary**: Common and accepted in your trade or freelance field.
2. **Necessary**: Helpful, appropriate, and beneficial for carrying out your professional activities.

## The Audit-Proof 3-Point Checklist

To guarantee that your expense claims survive tax scrutiny:

1. **Proof of Purchase**: Bank or card statement matching the ledger entry.
2. **Vendor Receipt**: Invoice showing line items, dates, and sales tax.
3. **Professional Justification**: A brief note explaining why this expense was incurred for your work.
