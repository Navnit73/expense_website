## What Qualifies as a Deductible Business Expense?

In most jurisdictions (including the US IRS, UK HMRC, Australian ATO, and Canadian CRA), a deductible expense must satisfy two fundamental tests:

1. **Ordinary**: Common and accepted in your trade or line of business.
2. **Necessary**: Helpful, appropriate, and beneficial for carrying out your business activities.

## The Audit-Proof 3-Point Checklist

To guarantee that your expense claims survive tax scrutiny:

1. **Proof of Purchase**: Bank or credit card statement matching the ledger entry.
2. **Vendor Receipt**: Invoice showing line items, dates, and sales tax.
3. **Business Justification**: A brief note explaining why this expense was incurred.
