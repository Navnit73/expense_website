The subscription economy has made recurring billing the default model for everything from household utilities and streaming services to professional software tools.

The average consumer pays for 4.2 subscriptions they have completely forgotten about, wasting an estimated $420 to $960 annually. For households and freelancers, unmonitored recurring subscriptions can silently drain thousands from your savings.

---

## What Are "Zombie" Expenses?

A **Zombie Expense** is an automated recurring charge that renews on auto-pay without providing ongoing utility or conscious awareness:

- **Free-Trial Conversions**: That $19.99/mo PDF editor or video tool signed up for a single task.
- **Seat Abandonment**: Cloud software licenses assigned to team members who have since left the organization.
- **Silent Price Hikes**: Subscriptions that increased from $9.99 to $14.99 with only a buried email notice.
- **Duplicate Utility**: Paying for both Spotify and Apple Music, or Dropbox and Google Drive simultaneously.
