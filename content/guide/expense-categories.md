The secret to powerful financial analytics is clean categorization. If your categories are too broad (e.g. just *Miscellaneous* and *Living*), you gain no useful insights. If your categories are too granular (e.g. *Organic Whole Milk* vs *Oat Milk*), logging becomes a chore.

Here is the recommended standard taxonomy for personal and business finances.

---

## Recommended Personal Expense Categories

### 1. Essential Fixed Costs (Needs)
- **Housing**: Rent, mortgage principal & interest, HOA fees, property taxes.
- **Utilities**: Electricity, water, natural gas, trash collection, high-speed internet.
- **Transportation**: Car payment, fuel, auto insurance, public transit passes, routine maintenance.
- **Groceries**: Supermarket food purchases, household essentials.
- **Healthcare**: Health insurance premiums, prescription copays, dental & vision visits.

### 2. Discretionary Spending (Wants)
- **Dining Out & Cafes**: Restaurants, takeout, coffee shops, bars.
- **Entertainment & Leisure**: Movie tickets, concerts, gaming, sports events.
- **Subscriptions & Digital Media**: Netflix, Spotify, cloud storage, fitness apps.
- **Shopping & Apparel**: Clothing, footwear, personal electronics, home decor.
- **Travel & Vacations**: Flights, hotels, car rentals, vacation dining.

## 3 Rules for Creating Custom Categories in Expenseliy

1. **The 80/20 Rule**: 80% of your transactions should cleanly fit into 8 to 12 core categories.
2. **Use Tags for Sub-dimensions**: Rather than creating *Software - Marketing* and *Software - Engineering*, use category `Software & SaaS` with tags `#marketing` or `#engineering`.
3. **Audit Monthly**: Review uncategorized items at the end of the month to spot emerging categories (e.g. *Pet Care* or *Equipment Upgrades*).
